Privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-20

The short version

  • Everything you type into a FridgeNote tool stays in your browser. We don't receive it, store it, or see it.
  • If you leave "Save a draft on this device" on, your answers are kept in your browser's local storage so you can reuse them. You can turn this off or press Clear at any time.
  • We may use Google services (such as Google AdSense and Google Search Console) that use cookies. Details are below.

Information you type into the tools

Our tools run entirely in your web browser. Names, phone numbers, addresses, pet details, Wi-Fi passwords, and anything else you enter are processed on your device to build the preview and the printout. This information is not sent to us or to any third party.

Drafts are saved with your browser's local storage only when the save option is on. Clearing your browser data, turning the option off, or pressing Clear removes them.

Advertising and cookies

We may show ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings, or visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Analytics and server logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. We use these only in aggregate and do not try to identify visitors.

Children

FridgeNote is meant for adults, such as parents, pet owners, and hosts. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
