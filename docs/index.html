Why a printed sheet still beats a text message

Texts get buried. A sheet on the fridge is the first thing a sitter sees, it works when their phone is charging, and it's easy to point to when you're on the phone together. Send a PDF copy too, and they have it both ways.

How it works

  1. Pick a sheet and fill in what applies. Blank boxes stay off the page.
  2. Watch the preview update as you type. It shrinks the text to keep everything on one page.
  3. Print it, or save it as a PDF to send.

Your information stays with you

Every sheet is made inside your browser. Phone numbers, addresses, and Wi-Fi passwords are never uploaded. If you want to reuse a sheet, you can save a draft on your own device. Read more.
