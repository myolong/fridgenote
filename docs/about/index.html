About FridgeNote

FridgeNote helps you write down everything someone needs to know while they look after your home, your pets, or your kids, and print it on one page.

Why it exists

Before a trip, most of us end up writing the same note by hand: how much the dog eats, when the cat gets her pill, which day the trash goes out, the Wi-Fi password. It's easy to forget something, and messy handwriting doesn't help at 7 AM.

FridgeNote turns that note into a simple form. You fill in what applies, see the finished page right away, and print it. Next time, you change the dates and print again.

How we handle your information

Every sheet is built inside your own browser. What you type is not uploaded to our server, because we don't have one that receives it. If you choose to save a draft, it stays in your browser on your device. You can read the details on our privacy page.

What we don't do

  • We don't give veterinary, medical, legal, or insurance advice. The sheets have boxes for your instructions, written the way your vet, doctor, or local rules tell you.
  • We don't recommend specific products, sitters, or services.
  • We don't ask you to sign up, and there is no paid version.

How the site is paid for

FridgeNote may show ads on its pages to cover hosting and development. Ads never appear on the printed sheet.
