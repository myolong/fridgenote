Pet sitter instructions

Fill in the boxes, check the preview, and print one page for the fridge. Works for dogs, cats, and most other pets.

What you type stays in this browser. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Fill in what applies. Empty boxes don't show up on the printed sheet, so skip anything that doesn't fit your pet.
  2. Check the preview. It's exactly what will print. The note under it tells you if everything fits on one page.
  3. Print or save as PDF. Stick it on the fridge and send a copy to your sitter's phone.

What sitters say they actually need

Most mix-ups happen around four things. If you only have five minutes, fill these in first.

A few tips from pet owners

  • Write medicine exactly as your vet told you. Copy the label. Don't round or guess.
  • Mention the "normal weird" stuff. A cat hiding under the bed on day one, or a dog who won't eat while you're gone, can scare a new sitter. One line saves a worried phone call.
  • Add a backup contact who lives nearby. If the sitter can't reach you, they need someone who can get to the house.
  • Put the carrier and leash location on the sheet. In an emergency, finding the carrier is often the slowest part.
  • Do a short trial visit. Walk through the sheet with your sitter before you leave. They'll spot what's missing.

Going away often? Leave "Save a draft on this device" on. Next time, just change the dates and print.

Questions

Is this really free?
Yes. There's no account, no watermark, and no paid version. Print as many copies as you like.
Where does my information go?
Nowhere. The sheet is built inside your browser. If you leave "Save a draft on this device" on, your answers are stored only in this browser so you can reuse them next trip. Turn it off, or press Clear, to remove them.
Can I save it as a PDF instead of printing?
Yes. Press Print, then choose "Save as PDF" as the printer. You can text or email the PDF to your sitter.
What if it doesn't fit on one page?
The text shrinks automatically. If it still doesn't fit, you'll see a red note under the preview. Shorten the longest boxes, or move extra details to the Notes box.
Should I write my door code on it?
We'd rather you didn't. A sheet on the fridge can be seen by anyone who walks in, and sometimes from a window. Give codes to your sitter in person or by text.
