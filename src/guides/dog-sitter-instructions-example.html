It's easier to write instructions when you can see an example. Here's one for Max, a 6-year-old beagle mix who lives with a cat named Luna. Each section explains why it's there.

How to reach us

Owner: Sam & Alex · (555) 123-4567

Staying at: Lakeview Inn, Traverse City

Best contact: Text anytime. Call if it's urgent.

Saying "text first" tells the sitter they won't be bothering you, which makes them more likely to reach out early.

About Max

Food: 1 cup dry food (bin in pantry)

Feeding: 7 AM and 6 PM

Treats: 2 small treats a day

Medicine: 1 allergy pill with breakfast. Hide it in a bit of cheese.

Good to know: Barks at the mail carrier, then settles down. Pulls on the leash near squirrels.

Never: Grapes, chocolate, off-leash walks

Notice how specific the amounts are. "Feed him twice a day" invites guessing. The "Good to know" line is there so a new sitter doesn't panic when Max barks.

Daily routine

TimeWhat to do
7:00 AMFeed Max and Luna. Max's pill. Short walk.
12:00 PMLet Max out in the backyard. Fresh water.
6:00 PMFeed both. 20-minute walk.
9:30 PMScoop litter. Max into his crate.

Four lines cover the whole day. If your sitter only visits twice, write the routine for their visits, not your usual day.

Walks and bedtime

Walks: 20 minutes, morning and evening. Leash and bags by the front door.

Bedtime: Max sleeps in his crate in the laundry room.

If something goes wrong

Vet: Oak Street Animal Hospital · (555) 222-3333

24-hr clinic: Northside 24-Hour Pet ER · (555) 999-0000

Backup: Neighbor Jordan · (555) 444-5555

Carrier: Hall closet, top shelf

The backup contact matters more than people think. If your sitter can't reach you, they need someone who can actually get to the house.

The printed version

Adapting it for your dog

  • Puppies: add potty times and crate rules. Puppies need more breaks than adult dogs.
  • Older dogs: mention stairs, joint medicine, and how far they can comfortably walk.
  • Anxious dogs: describe what calms them, like a favorite blanket or the radio on.
  • Escape artists: say which gates and doors to double-check.
