Trips go better when you're not wondering whether you left the stove on. This list is meant to be practical, not exhaustive. Skip what doesn't apply.

The week before

  • Ask a neighbor, friend, or house sitter to check on the house, and agree on how often.
  • Hold your mail if no one will collect it. In the US, the Postal Service lets you request a hold online.
  • Pause or reschedule deliveries and subscriptions.
  • Refill prescriptions you'll need while you're away.
  • Arrange care for pets and plants.
  • Check the trash and recycling calendar so bins don't sit at the curb for a week.

The day before

  • Empty the fridge of anything that will spoil.
  • Run the dishwasher and take out the trash.
  • Charge devices and pack chargers.
  • Set lights on timers, or ask your sitter to switch lamps on each visit.
  • Print the house sitter sheet and leave it somewhere obvious.

The last hour

  • Set the thermostat. In cold weather, keep the heat on low so pipes don't freeze.
  • Unplug small appliances.
  • Check the stove, oven, and iron are off.
  • Close and lock windows, then doors. Check the garage.
  • Take one last look at your ID, tickets, and wallet.
