How to use it

  1. Write a short welcome and add your Wi-Fi. The cover gets a QR code guests can scan to join.
  2. Fill in the questions guests ask most. Heat, TV, coffee, trash, and checkout. Skip anything you don't need.
  3. Print double-sided and put it somewhere obvious. A binder or a clear sleeve on the kitchen counter works well.

Tips from hosts

  • Answer the questions you get by message. If three guests asked how the TV works, it belongs in the book.
  • Keep checkout short. Four or five simple tasks feel fair. A long list can sour the end of a nice stay.
  • Write local tips in your own words. "Get the cherry scone" is more useful than a list of restaurant names.
  • Keep codes out of print. Send door and lockbox codes by message, and change them between stays.
  • Check your local rules separately. Short-term rental rules, taxes, and safety requirements vary by city. This tool doesn't cover them.
